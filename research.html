In Progress Master’s Thesis Privacy & AI

Personalized Privacy Guardrails for LLM Interactions: The Role of Terminology Adapted to User’s Prior Privacy Knowledge

Master’s Thesis · Ludwig-Maximilians-University Munich · Submission: 27 October 2026

My master’s thesis designs and evaluates a personalized privacy intervention system for conversations with large language models. The system detects potentially sensitive information while users are writing their prompts and provides adaptive, real-time warnings before disclosure. The core research question is how privacy warnings can be adapted to a user’s prior privacy knowledge – and how this affects disclosure behaviour, acceptance, perceived autonomy, reactance and compliance in a naturalistic user study.

Published Conference Paper Bachelor’s Thesis University of Bayreuth’s Thesis HICSS 2022

Towards Designing a Mobile Stress Coping Assistant

Marco Schmidt, Michelle Berger, Lea Görl, Stefanie Lahmer, Henner Gimpel · Proceedings of the 55th Hawaii International Conference on System Sciences (HICSS), 2022

A peer-reviewed publication that emerged from my bachelor’s thesis (the myStress project). Building on existing research and mobile apps for stress coping, the paper proposes the design of a mobile coping assistant that uses multimodal sensor data to help individuals detect and cope with stress.

This work builds directly on my bachelor’s thesis, “Towards Designing and Evaluating a Mobile App for Providing Individualized Coping Assistance Against Stress”.

Seminar Paper Ethics & AI Digital Design

Give me your undivided Attention! – Balancing Persuasion and Ethics in Digital Design

Seminar “Ethics of Artificial Intelligence” · LMU Munich, 2024

A seminar paper on the ethics of persuasive digital design. It examines the attention economy and persuasive as well as AI-driven design techniques, and explores where persuasion crosses the line into manipulation. Drawing on value-sensitive design and existing guidelines, it discusses how digital products can stay engaging while respecting users’ autonomy and freedom of choice.
